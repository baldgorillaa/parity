PARITY
PLAYER'S GUIDE
1. WHAT IS PARITY?
PARITY is a math speed game built on a single concept: even or odd? You see two numbers and an operation. Decide whether the result is even (press 0) or odd (press 1) — as fast as possible.
No calculating the exact answer. Just parity.
2. THE CORE RULES
Addition
Only the last digit of each number matters:
12 + 340 EVEN(2+4=6 → even)
13 + 241 ODD(3+4=7 → odd)
11 + 330 EVEN(1+3=4 → even)
💡 Shortcut: even+even=even, odd+odd=even, even+odd=odd. You never need the full result.
Multiplication (ADVANCE mode only)
A product is even unless both numbers are odd:
12 × 350 EVEN(12 is even)
13 × 271 ODD(both odd)
💡 Shortcut for multiplication: if you see ANY even number, the answer is EVEN. Only odd×odd = ODD.
3. GAME MODES
ModeHow it works
NORMALTwo independent random numbers. Addition only. Each question is fresh.
STACKThe result of your last answer becomes the hidden first operand of the next question. Track the running chain in your head — the screen shows ? for it.
ADVANCEAddition and multiplication appear randomly. In INFINITE, each question is independent. In QUICK, results carry forward (STACK mechanic).
⚠ In STACK: the hidden operand is NOT a mystery — it's the result you just calculated. You must remember it yourself. That's the challenge.
4. PLAY TYPES
INFINITE
One wrong answer ends the run. Maximum 150 questions. Difficulty increases at question 76 — numbers become 3 digits. Your score is your streak and the time you lasted.

⚠ The clock never stops in QUICK mode. The penalty is deducted from your remaining time.
5. CONTROLS
Desktop
Default: 0 = EVEN, 1 = ODD. Customisable in Settings — set them to any keyboard key.
Mobile
Tap the large 0 EVEN or 1 ODD buttons at the bottom of the screen.
Pause
Tap ⏸ during gameplay, or press P / Escape on desktop. The timer freezes while paused.
6. SETTINGS GUIDE
Step-by-step: Custom Keys
1. Open Settings from the main menu.
2. Tap the button next to EVEN KEY — it will blink amber and say ...
3. Press any key on your keyboard. That key is now your EVEN answer.
4. Repeat for ODD KEY.
5. Changes save automatically.
Recommended: use adjacent keys like Z / X or A / S for faster, less fatiguing reactions.
Sound
Toggle in Settings. If sound doesn't work immediately, interact with the screen once — browsers require a user gesture before playing audio.
Fullscreen
Settings → tap FULLSCREEN → TOGGLE. On mobile, use your browser's "Add to Home Screen" for a near-native fullscreen experience. On iOS Safari: Share → Add to Home Screen.
Reset Scores
Clears all personal best records stored on this device. This cannot be undone.
7. PRO TIPS
🔢 Last-digit rule: For addition, only the last digit matters. 357 + 482 → 7+2=9 → ODD. This cuts your mental workload dramatically.
✖ Multiply in 1 second: Spot any even number → answer EVEN immediately. Only scan both if you see two odd numbers.
🧠 STACK strategy: Don't track the full number — only track whether it's even (0) or odd (1). Then apply the parity rules for the new operand.
⚡ QUICK accuracy beats speed: A 0.7s penalty is worse than pausing 0.5s to be sure. Aim for >95% accuracy before optimising for pace.
🎯 Warm up: Start with NORMAL QUICK 20 to calibrate your reaction time. Then extend to 50 and 100 once you're consistent.
🏆 INFINITE grind: The hardest part is the transition at Q76 when numbers go 3-digit. Practice NORMAL INFINITE until you clear Q75 reliably before moving to STACK.
